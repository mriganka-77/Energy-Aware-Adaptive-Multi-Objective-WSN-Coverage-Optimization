Theoretical Formulation & Mathematical Model

Multi-objective Pareto vector, energy-aware displacement constraints, and first-order radio dissipation equations.

System Problem Formulation

Random sensor deployment in wireless sensor networks inevitably produces non-uniform spatial topologies, manifesting as high-density clusters (redundant sensing overlap) and unmonitored coverage holes. While mobile sensor actuators can theoretically reposition nodes into regular lattices, unconstrained physical relocation rapidly depletes onboard electrochemical batteries (typically E0 = 0.50 Joules).

EA-VVF-MOPSO addresses this trade-off by coupling Multi-Objective Particle Swarm Optimization with bounded Voronoi territory guidance and Virtual Force repulsion. An Energy-Aware Controller enforces zero-mobility barriers on energy-depleted nodes, preserving power for mission-critical downstream packet forwarding under the Hybrid LEACH-PEGASIS protocol.

Multi-Objective Fitness Vector

The optimization objective maps a multi-criteria vector balancing coverage quality against actuator displacement energy:

max F = [ w_C · CR - w_O · OR - w_H · HR + w_E · E_res + w_K · K_conn - w_M · M_cost ]

Non-dominated candidate solutions are sorted into a Pareto archive using crowding-distance truncation to preserve diversity across the objective frontier.

First-Order Radio Energy Dissipation

Energy dissipation per k-bit transmission over distance d follows the Heinzelman free-space and multipath model:

E_tx(k, d) = k · E_elec + (d < d0 ? k · ε_fs · d² : k · ε_mp · d⁴)

Where cross-over threshold d0 = sqrt(ε_fs / ε_mp) = 87.7m. Cluster Heads incur additional data aggregation dissipation EDA = 5 nJ/bit/signal.
